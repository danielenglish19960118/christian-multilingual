Parallel English & Multilingual Translations

1. Methodology of Comparative Translation Analysis

Comparing formal equivalence (word-for-word) translations alongside functional equivalence (thought-for-thought) translations allows language scholars and Bible students to discern syntactic structure and nuance.

2. Exemplar Comparative Verse Matrix (John 1:1)

[Greek Nestle-Aland 28th] Ἐν ἀρχῇ ἦν ὁ λόγος, καὶ ὁ λόγος ἦν πρὸς τὸν θεόν, καὶ θεὸς ἦν ὁ λόγος. [English Standard Version - ESV] In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God. [Chinese Union Version - CUV 和合本] 太初有道，道與神同在，道就是神。 [Latin Vulgate] In principio erat Verbum, et Verbum erat apud Deum, et Deus erat Verbum.
