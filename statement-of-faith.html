Statement of Faith

Core Historical & Theological Confession

We unite in historical, orthodox Christian faith, affirming the foundational truths revealed in Scripture:

  • The Authority of Scripture: We believe the 66 books of the Old and New Testaments are verbally inspired by God, inerrant in their original autographs, and serve as our supreme and final authority.
  • The Triune God: We confess one sovereign God eternally existing in three co-equal persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.
  • The Person and Work of Christ: We believe Jesus Christ is fully God and fully man, born of a virgin, lived a sinless life, died vicariously for our sins, bodily resurrected, and ascended to the right hand of the Father.
  • Spiritual Unity: Salvation is received by grace through faith in Christ alone, uniting believers into one body across every tribe, language, and nation.
