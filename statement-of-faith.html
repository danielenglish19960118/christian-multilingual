Statement of Faith & Theological Foundations

Historical Evangelical & Orthodox Confession

We unite in historical, orthodox Christian faith, affirming the foundational truths revealed in Holy Scripture as articulated across historical creeds (Apostles' Creed, Nicene Creed):

  • 1. Divine Inspiration of Scripture: The 66 canonical books of the Old and New Testaments are verbally inspired by God, inerrant in their original autographs, and serve as our supreme and final authority for faith, doctrine, and practice (2 Timothy 3:16-17).
  • 2. The Holy Trinity: We confess one sovereign God eternally existing in three co-equal, co-eternal, distinct Persons: God the Father, God the Son (Jesus Christ), and God the Holy Spirit (Matthew 28:19).
  • 3. Jesus Christ the Savior: We believe Jesus Christ is fully God and fully man, conceived by the Holy Spirit, born of the Virgin Mary, lived a sinless life, died substitutionally on the cross for our redemption, bodily resurrected on the third day, ascended into heaven, and will return in power and glory (John 1:1-14, 1 Corinthians 15:3-4).
  • 4. Salvation by Grace Through Faith: Justification is an act of God's sovereign grace received exclusively through personal faith in Jesus Christ, apart from human merit or works (Ephesians 2:8-9).
  • 5. Universal Unity of Believers: The universal Church comprises all who have been regenerated by the Holy Spirit, forming one spiritual body across every nation, language, and culture (Revelation 7:9).
