Original Language Lexicons & Grammatical Concordances

1. Primary Lexicographical Resources

Accurate exegesis demands rigorous consultation of primary lexicons to avoid historical and root fallacies. Key references include:

  • Brown-Driver-Briggs (BDB): Standard lexicon for Old Testament Hebrew and Aramaic.
  • Thayer's Greek Lexicon / BDAG: Authoritative lexicon for New Testament Koine Greek parsing.
  • Strong's Exhaustive Concordance: Numerical index mapping every translated word to its underlying Greek/Hebrew lemma.
