λόγος (Logos)

Definition: Something said, including the thought; reason, word, divine expression or personified Word of God.

Contextual Exegesis: In John 1:1, *ho logos* refers eternally to the second person of the Trinity prior to incarnation, embodying divine rationality, creative agency, and supreme revelation.

Analysis of papyri, uncials, minuscules, and text types (Alexandrian, Byzantine, Western).

Alexandrian Text-Type (e.g., Codex Sinaiticus, Vaticanus)

Characterized by concise, early readings dating back to the 2nd–3rd centuries. Highly prioritized in modern critical editions (NA28 / UBS5).

Byzantine Text-Type (Majority Text)

Represented by the vast majority of medieval minuscule manuscripts. Smooth and full in expression, forming the basis of the Textus Receptus.
