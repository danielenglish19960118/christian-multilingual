Passage Selector:
Strong's G3056 Noun, Masculine Nominative Singular

λόγος (Logos)

Lexical Definition: Something said (including the thought); by implication, a topic, subject, or reasoning; personified as the divine Word.

Syntactical & Theological Exegesis:

In John 1:1, the anarthrous use of 'Theos' contrasted with the arthrous 'ho logos' emphasizes qualitative divine essence rather than strict identity of person, safeguarding orthodox Trinitarian distinction while affirming absolute deity.

Textual Criticism & Manuscript Witness Distribution

Comparative analysis of Alexandrian, Byzantine, Western, and Caesarean text-types across Greek uncials and minuscules.

1. Alexandrian Text-Type (P75, Codex Sinaiticus א, Codex Vaticanus B)

Dated to the 2nd–4th centuries. Noted for brevity, strict accuracy, and careful scribal preservation without harmonization tendencies.

2. Byzantine Text-Type (Majority Text & Textus Receptus)

Comprises over 80% of surviving medieval minuscules. Highly polished, liturgical, and expansive, forming the foundation of the King James Bible.
