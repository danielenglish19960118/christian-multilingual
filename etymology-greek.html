Koine Greek Manuscript Studies (New Testament)

1. Koine Greek Morphological Precision

New Testament Koine Greek employs rich inflections, verbal tenses (Aorist, Perfect, Present), and grammatical cases to express precision in theological truth.

2. Key Lexical Exegesis

  • Agape (ἀγάπη - Strong's G26): Unconditional, self-sacrificing love originating from God.
  • Logos (λόγος - Strong's G3056): The divine Word, ultimate expression, and person of Jesus Christ (John 1:1).
  • Koinonia (κοινωνία - Strong's G2842): Fellowship, intimate spiritual participation, and mutual sharing.
